A few things to know

Before you register.

Do I need experience?

Beginner classes are designed for students starting from the basics. The level is shown on each class.

How do I know when registration is open?

Open classes show a Register button and current fee. You can also join the mailing list for new dates.

How do I pay?

Open class registrations are paid securely online through Paystack.

Where can I find the beginner makeup guide?

The detailed guide and tutorial link are available on the Student Resources page.
