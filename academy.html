Inside the academy

Cisca Academy turns the techniques used behind the chair into structured lessons. Students learn how to prep, choose tools, work through each stage of a look and understand why each step matters.

Classes can cover makeup, brows and lashes, nails, hair installation and styling. Class dates, fees and open registration are controlled directly by Cisca.

Before you register.

Do I need experience?

Beginner classes are designed for students starting from the basics. The level is shown on each class.

How do I know when registration is open?

Open classes show a Register button and current fee. You can also join the mailing list for new dates.

How do I pay?

Open class registrations are paid securely online through Paystack.
