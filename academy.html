CM Beginner Makeup Guide

Keep this as your quick reference after class.

01

Eyebrows

Spoolie brush

Comb through the brows first so you can see the natural direction and shape.

Razor blade

Remove excess growth carefully to refine the brow shape.

Clean

Use tissue wipes to clean the brow and surrounding skin before product.

Pencil

Fill with brown or black pencil to the preferred thickness, keeping the front softer.

Concealer

Carve the brow with a lighter shade around the tail and a slightly deeper shade toward the inner brow.

Blend

Soften concealer edges until there are no obvious lines around the brow.

02

Skin and complexion

Cleanse

Clean with micellar water and cotton pads. For deeper bridal prep, follow with an oil-based cleanser and remove thoroughly.

Tone

Use toner when appropriate for the client’s skin and routine.

Moisturize

Apply evenly with clean hands or a clean flat brush and allow it to settle.

Prime

Choose pore-filling, mattifying or hydrating primer based on skin type.

Foundation

Test the shade first, mix when needed, then blend until the transition is seamless.

Contour and bronzer

Use contour for structure and bronzer for warmth. Adjust placement to face shape and complexion.

Conceal

Brighten the under-eye, nose, forehead, chin and other highlight points when the look calls for it.

Set

Use loose or translucent powder on highlights, then pressed powder where the complexion needs it.

03

Eyes and lashes

Prime

Use eyeshadow primer or concealer to create an even lid base.

Transition

Blend a transition pigment first, then build colour gradually.

Shimmer

Place shimmer or glitter on the inner corner or centre depending on the look.

Cut crease

Carve with concealer, set the base, add pigment and blend the edge into the transition shade.

Liner

Define the lash line, wing and waterline as required.

Lashes

Measure, trim, apply adhesive, allow it to become tacky, then place carefully from the outer corner inward.

04

Lips

Prep

Start with lip balm so the surface is smooth and comfortable.

Line

Use a pencil to create definition, especially for nude combinations.

Colour

Apply lip stain or lipstick and blend neatly with a lip brush.

Finish

Add gloss for shine or leave the lip matte depending on the final look.

05

Touch-up and finish

Pressed powder

Refine the complexion and reset any areas that need it.

Finishing spray

Lock in the completed makeup with a suitable finishing spray.

06
