01

Eyebrows

Spoolie brush

Comb through the brows first so you can see the natural direction and shape.

Razor blade

Remove excess growth carefully to refine the brow shape.

Clean

Use tissue wipes to clean the brow and the skin around it before product.

Pencil

Fill the brow with a brown or black pencil to the preferred thickness. Keep the front softer than the tail.

Concealer

Carve the brow using a lighter shade around the tail and a slightly deeper shade toward the inner brow for a softer transition.

Blend

Use a brow brush to soften concealer edges until there are no visible harsh lines.

02

Skin and complexion

Cleanse

Clean the face with micellar water and cotton pads. For deeper bridal prep, follow with an oil-based cleanser and wipe away thoroughly.

Tone

Use toner when appropriate for the client’s skin and routine.

Moisturize

Apply moisturizer evenly with clean hands or a clean flat brush. Give it time to settle into the skin.

Prime

Choose pore-filling, mattifying or hydrating primer based on the skin type. Blend from face through the neck area where needed.

Foundation

Mix on a palette when needed, test the shade first, then blend foundation until the transition is seamless.

Contour and bronzer

Use contour to create structure and bronzer to bring warmth. Placement and depth should change with complexion and face shape.

Conceal

For a more defined glam, brighten under the eyes, bridge of nose, forehead, chin and other highlight points, then blend once the product has set slightly.

Set

Set highlighted areas with loose or translucent powder, then use pressed powder to set the complexion where needed.

03

Eyeshadow and lashes

Prime the lid

Use eyeshadow primer or concealer to create an even base before pigment.

Transition

Blend a transition pigment first, then build the chosen colours gradually.

Shimmer

Place shimmer or glitter on the inner corner or centre of the lid depending on the look.

Cut crease

Carve with concealer using a flat or angled brush, set the base, add pigment, then blend the edge into the transition shade.

Liner

Use pencil, pigment or liner to define the lash line, wing and waterline as required.

Lashes

Measure false lashes, trim to fit, apply adhesive, allow it to become tacky, then place from the outer corner inward.

04

Lips

Lip balm

Moisturize first so the lip surface is comfortable and smooth.

Line

Use a brow or dark lip pencil for definition, especially when creating a nude combination.

Colour

Apply lip stain or lipstick, then blend cleanly with a lip brush.

Finish

Add gloss for shine or leave the lip matte depending on the final look.

05

Touch-up and finish

Pressed powder

Use pressed powder where the complexion needs final setting or refinement.

Finishing spray

Lock in the completed makeup with a suitable finishing spray.
